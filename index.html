Who We Are

Fallen Worlds.
Unbroken Hope.

Iron Veil Press publishes tabletop roleplaying adventures that take Christian themes seriously — not as sermon illustrations, but as living, breathing narrative bones beneath every dungeon, every warlord, every moment of grace under impossible pressure.

Our modules are built on the D&D 5e System Reference Document (SRD 5.1), released under Creative Commons CC-BY-4.0. That means you keep every dollar you spend here in exchange for adventures that cost us everything to imagine.

⚔

Spiritual Warfare as Encounter Design

Every boss, every faction, every cursed artifact is a theology lesson written in monster stats and blood.

✝

Redemption Arcs, Not Easy Grace

Characters in our worlds earn their salvation. Sanctification is mechanical. Fallen heroes can rise — but it costs them.

📖

Lore Rooted in Scripture

Our cosmologies, faction histories, and villain motivations draw from the full canon — Genesis to Revelation, Apocrypha and all.
