Know the rules boundary

Ordinary MVP play uses prepared Phrase cards with printed DCs and Strain costs, one roll, a six-box Strain track, named Conditions, Failure Echoes, Force It at 6 Strain, and Short/Long Rest recovery.

World Drift scores, glyph arithmetic, Backlash tables, and stacked environmental modifiers are not required to run this public build. Keep advanced material separate unless it is explicitly labeled as an optional trial.

After the session

Tell us where a Phrase felt clear, where Strain created useful pressure, where a Failure Echo surprised the table, and where the story asked for a better tool. Session summaries, rules observations, and respectful critique are welcome. Please do not include private player information.
