# Welcome to Threl

## A public introduction from Iron Veil Press

**The road to Threl is open.**

Threl is a grimdark Christian tabletop role-playing setting about faith, reality, survival, and the cost of preserving what remains. It is a world where the boundaries between spiritual truth and physical existence have grown perilously thin, and where communities must work together to keep the world from unraveling.

## What is inside

This free public introduction gives a first view of the world of Threl:

- The failure of the Grand Monochord during the Great Decoherence.
- Dabar, the language bound to the structure of existence.
- Anchors, the cities that endure through the continual care of their people.
- The Fray, where definitions weaken and the world becomes uncertain.
- Scarred Nodes and the dangerous growth known as the Green Rot.
- The Three Orders and their different approaches to preservation, virtue, and discernment.
- The Unchained, a collective of rogue Dabar engineers, exiled Resonators, dissident scholars, and frontier technicians.
- Story possibilities for pilgrims, scholars, clergy, artisans, scouts, exiles, and travelers who must decide what is worth preserving.

## A world held together by vow, memory, and song

The age of Threl is marked by the Great Decoherence. Since the failure of the Grand Monochord, reality no longer feels entirely fixed. Mountains still rise, rivers still run, and fire still burns—but none of these things are taken for granted.

The setting is built for stories of haunted wilderness, tense city politics, theological conflict, dangerous discovery, and difficult moral choices. Its central question is not simply whether the characters can survive. It is what they owe one another when the world itself cannot be trusted to remain whole.

## Dabar: the language beneath the world

Dabar is not ordinary spellcraft. It is a way of engaging with the underlying order of reality: the hidden pattern by which matter, motion, memory, and meaning hold together.

A phrase may reinforce a boundary, preserve a fragile object, guide a living process, or reveal a truth hidden beneath appearances. The greater the change demanded of reality, the greater the resistance and danger. Words are not harmless in Threl.

## The places beyond the light

The settled cities of Threl are called Anchors. They are places where physical and spiritual orders meet in a fragile balance. Their halls, towers, inscriptions, and rituals help maintain regions in which ordinary laws can continue to function.

Beyond the last dependable light lies the Fray. Distance may deceive. Time may fold. Familiar things may take on unfamiliar meanings. Travel there requires preparation, restraint, and company—and survival depends on attention, trust, and the courage to recognize when the world has changed.

## Stories waiting on the road

A Threl campaign may begin in a stable city or at the edge of a Scarred Node. Characters might be pilgrims, mercenaries, scholars, clergy, artisans, scouts, exiles, or people who simply have nowhere else to go.

They may be asked to recover a lost inscription, escort a lantern caravan, investigate a new wound in reality, settle a dispute between Orders, or uncover the truth behind a place that should not exist.

The deepest questions are personal:

- What do you hold onto when the world forgets its own shape?
- What does faith require when certainty is gone?
- What would you risk to preserve a single life, a single truth, or a single light?
