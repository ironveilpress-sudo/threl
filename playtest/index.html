Know the rules boundary.

Ordinary MVP play uses prepared Phrase cards with printed DCs and Strain costs, one roll, a six-box Strain track, named Conditions, Failure Echoes, Force It at 6 Strain, and Short/Long Rest recovery.

Not ordinary MVP authority: World Drift scores, glyph arithmetic, Backlash tables, and stacked environmental modifiers. Those may appear in design notes or an explicitly labeled advanced trial, but they are not required to run this release.

Public boundary: this package does not include secret Warden lore, private Frey Wars history, internal audits, or unreleased canon decisions.
